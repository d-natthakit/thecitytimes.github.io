Cover Story

UK Police Open Sexual Offense Probe into Comedian David Walliams

C1 · 3 min

Police in London have launched an official investigation into serious sexual offense allegations against British comedian and children's author David Walliams. The Metropolitan Police confirmed on Friday that they received multiple reports of past sexual offenses involving a man in his 50s. Detective Superintendent Mike Cagney urged anyone affected by similar experiences to come forward and speak with officers.

The official police inquiry follows joint investigative reports published by ITV News and The Guardian. The media outlets released an hourlong video documentary titled David Walliams: Power and Silence. In their reporting, several women accused Walliams of sexual misconduct over a 20-year period dating back to 2003. One woman alleged that Walliams sexually assaulted her at his London home when she was 15 years old, while others claimed he groomed them while they were teenagers.

The investigative reports also highlighted allegations of workplace sexual harassment involving female colleagues at his former employer, book publisher HarperCollins, as well as inappropriate behavior on the television show Britain's Got Talent, where Walliams served as a judge. The published reports resulted from a three-year media investigation involving interviews with more than 100 people.

Walliams, who turned 55, firmly denies all allegations of wrongdoing. In a written statement to the media outlets, Walliams said that while a power imbalance is inevitable when one party is famous, abusing that power is something he never did. He maintained that he is not a sexual predator and has not broken the law. Walliams previously lost a High Court bid to block media outlets from naming him, after a judge ruled an injunction would interfere with media rights.

The Metropolitan Police stated that their current investigation is based on allegations separate from those reported by ITV and The Guardian. However, the public allegations have already caused severe professional consequences for the entertainer. The BBC confirmed it removed several shows featuring Walliams, including his famous sketch show Little Britain, from its services. His talent agency, Independent Talent Group, announced it no longer represents him in any capacity.

World in Brief

All Passengers Rescued After Mississippi River Cruise Ship Collision

B1+ · 2 min

Around 130 passengers were successfully rescued after a cruise ship collides with a cargo vessel on the Mississippi River near New Orleans on Friday. The riverboat, American Heritage, began taking on water and listing before tipping onto its side after being struck by the cargo ship Atlantic Asphalt. Officials confirmed 32 people were taken to hospital with non-critical injuries.

Emergency calls were received around 13:30 EST, prompting immediate action from first responders, police, and local tugboat operators. Authorities temporarily closed the Mississippi River to conduct the search effort. Local officials praised the rapid response, noting that zero people remained unaccounted for following the dramatic incident.

The United States Coast Guard reported that a loss of steering on one of the vessels appears to be the cause of the collision. The riverboat was completing an eight-day cruise when the crash occurred. Officials have launched a full investigation into the exact causes of the maritime accident.

Eisenkot Accuses Netanyahu of Racist Campaign Against Arab Minority

B1+ · 1 min

Israeli political leader Gadi Eisenkot has publicly accused caretaker Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu of leading a racist campaign against the country's Arab minority. Eisenkot criticized efforts to delegitimize Arab lawmakers after Netanyahu linked Arab-majority political parties to Hamas ahead of government-building talks.

Arab citizens make up over 20 percent of Israel's population, and two main Arab-majority parties won 19 parliamentary seats in recent elections. Eisenkot stated that Arab citizens are an integral part of Israeli society and condemned attempts to vilify them collectively. He argued that such political attacks violate basic principles of civic responsibility.

The political tension comes as Eisenkot attempts to form a new governing coalition after receiving the presidential mandate. However, right-wing allies have threatened to withdraw support if Arab parties are included in the final coalition agreement, creating further political uncertainty.

Thailand Today

Central Retail Reports Revenue Growth and Third-Quarter Profit Surge

B1+ · 2 min

Central Retail Corporation (CRC), a major Thai retail operator running store networks across Thailand and Vietnam, reported total revenue of 61.1 billion baht for the third quarter. This represents a 5 percent increase year-on-year. Core profit from continuing operations rose by 72 percent to reach 2.05 billion baht, exceeding two billion baht for the third consecutive year.

To sustain growth, CRC is accelerating its strategic plan in the final quarter. The company continues to enhance customer experiences by adopting modern artificial intelligence solutions across its store networks. Additionally, every corporate expansion project undergoes a strict financial assessment to maintain capital control, while the firm adopts electric delivery trucks and solar energy to reduce operational costs.

Central Department Store is also expanding its brand portfolio by introducing more than 25 new international brands during the upcoming festive season. Looking ahead, CRC plans to continue store renovations and new branch openings across both Thailand and Vietnam to capture rising seasonal consumer demand.

Abhisit Urges Transparency in Online Gambling Platform Inquiry

B1+ · 1 min

Democrat Party leader Abhisit Vejjajiva has urged the Department of Special Investigation, a Thai federal law enforcement agency, to handle its inquiry into the website Shuffle.com with complete transparency. He warned that a lack of clear explanation could undermine public confidence in the national financial system.

The case involves allegations regarding online gambling networks and money laundering activities linked to the family of a former political figure. Reports suggest the agency might not accept the case officially because the monetary amount falls short of the required 300 million baht threshold. Abhisit stated that if investigators decide not to pursue the matter, they must explain their legal reasons clearly.

The politician emphasized that Thailand is already facing public scrutiny regarding illicit capital and grey market activities. He added that state agencies must provide clear answers to parliamentary committees to protect economic stability and maintain public trust.

Feature · Cultures / Digital Life

Connecting Beyond Screens: Building Real Cultural Empathy in the Digital Age

B2 · 2 min

Modern professional life relies heavily on digital platforms to connect teams across continents. We send messages instantly, share project files in seconds, and attend virtual video meetings with colleagues from diverse international backgrounds. However, virtual connectivity does not automatically create deep cultural understanding. True global collaboration requires professional workers to look beyond digital communication tools and actively practice cultural empathy.

One common challenge in online workplaces is interpreting message tone without physical cues. In many Asian cultures, indirect communication is used to show respect, whereas Western colleagues often prefer direct feedback. When working through chat tools, these stylistic differences can cause minor misunderstandings. Taking time to learn how international colleagues communicate helps team members reach a mutual resolution during challenging projects.

Building genuine cross-cultural relationships also requires working within a clear communication framework. Professionals should remain curious about regional customs, public holidays, and local business etiquette. Instead of assuming everyone shares the same routine, adaptable workers adjust their expectations regarding working hours and response times. Showing consideration for international calendars builds long-term respect.

To facilitate better teamwork, remote workers can create casual virtual spaces for personal conversations. Brief informal chats before business meetings allow colleagues to share daily cultural experiences, local traditions, or personal stories. These small human connections make virtual interactions feel warmer and reduce the feeling of distance created by digital screens.

Navigating cultural differences takes mental stamina and patience. Digital tools help us exchange technical information quickly, but human relationships depend on active listening and open minds. By intentionally practicing cultural empathy online, modern professionals can transform global digital workspaces into truly inclusive, supportive environments.

Real English · Two Takes

Should teams require members to turn cameras on during routine virtual meetings?

C1 · 2 min

Visibility Builds Connection

Keeping cameras on during team calls creates essential visual engagement. Seeing colleagues' facial expressions helps team members connect personally and read non-verbal cues. Admittedly, constantly appearing on screen can feel tiring during long work sessions. However, visual interaction prevents people from multitasking and keeps discussions focused. Turning cameras on builds trust among remote team members and creates a real meeting environment.

Flexibility Respects Privacy

Mandatory camera policies put unnecessary pressure on employees working from home. People may have private home environments or poor internet connections that make video calls stressful. While seeing facial expressions is helpful, forcing people onto camera does not guarantee actual engagement. Workers can participate actively through voice chat and shared digital documents. Allowing video choice respects personal privacy and reduces online fatigue.

Thai Speaker's Trap

Word order & questions

Word Order in Questions

Why you did not turn on your camera during the meeting?

Why didn't you turn on your camera during the meeting?

Thai speakers often place the subject before the auxiliary verb in English questions, directly translating structures like ทำไมคุณไม่ (why you not). In English direct questions, the auxiliary verb must come before the subject.

Odd one out

  1. 1. (d) shallow — 'Scrutiny' implies deep and thorough inspection, so 'shallow' contradicts its core meaning.
  2. 2. (d) completely — 'Inevitable' is an adjective, so it usually collocates with nouns like result, change, or outcome, not an adverb.
  3. 3. (d) brightly — 'Brightly' is an adverb used for light or mood, not describing improper behavior or misconduct.
  4. 4. (d) decision — One 'makes' or 'reaches' a decision rather than 'undergoing' it.
  5. 5. (d) delicious — 'Delicious' describes food taste, whereas 'stamina' relates to physical or mental endurance.
  6. 6. (d) flying — 'Listing' refers to water vessels leaning to one side, not flying aircraft.

Error Identification

  1. UK Police open a sexual offense probe into comedian David Walliams. — 'Police' takes a plural verb in British English, and 'probe' needs an article.
  2. All passengers were rescued after a Mississippi River cruise ship collision. — The sentence structure requires a noun phrase ('collision') after the pre-positional setup.
  3. Eisenkot accuses Netanyahu of launching a racist campaign against the Arab minority. — Adding the participle 'launching' clarifies the action of starting a campaign.
  4. Central Retail reports revenue growth and a third-quarter profit surge. — Parallel structure requires a noun ('profit surge') to match 'revenue growth'.
  5. Abhisit urges transparency in the online gambling platform inquiry. — 'Inquiry' typically takes the preposition 'in' or 'into' rather than 'on' in this context.
