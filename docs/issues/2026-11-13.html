Cover Story

Pentagon Chief Awards Medal to Guard Who Demanded Silence at War Memorial

C1 · 2 min

US Defence Secretary Pete Hegseth has presented a military award to an army guard who publicly reprimanded him and his entourage for talking and laughing during a visit to one of America's most sacred war memorials. The incident occurred during a Veterans Day observance event at the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier in Arlington National Cemetery.

A video recording captured the defense secretary, his wife, and senior advisers talking, laughing, and posing for photographs in front of the historic site. Specialist Thomas Iglesias, an army sentinel on duty, stepped toward the group and shouted an order for all visitors to maintain absolute silence and respect. Arlington etiquette guidelines explicitly request that visitors speak quietly and avoid any disrespectful conduct.

Following the incident, Hegseth issued an official commendation to Iglesias. The Pentagon confirmed that the secretary presented the Joint Service Commendation Medal to the guard for the proper execution of his duties. Hegseth posted photographs of the presentation on social media, writing that he values strong standards and praising the soldier for enforcing rules at the memorial.

Chief Pentagon spokesman Sean Parnell publicly supported the guard's actions. Parnell stated that during the solemn occasion, the sentinel acted correctly and respectfully when reminding the group to maintain appropriate decorum. He added that the secretary and the entire department remain dedicated to honoring military sacrifices.

Specialist Iglesias serves as a guard with the 3rd US Infantry Regiment, a unit known as the Old Guard. This historic unit holds continuous vigil over the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier. The tomb contains the remains of an unidentified US service member killed in World War One and serves as a quiet national sanctuary to honor all missing and fallen military personnel.

Nvidia Search Begins for New Leader Following Unexpected Resignation

B1+ · 1 min

Nvidia has launched a search for a permanent chief executive officer after co-founder Jensen Huang resigned amid personal conduct allegations. The technology company immediately named its chief financial officer, Colette Kress, as interim chief executive while the board considers potential permanent replacements.

Huang had led Nvidia since founding it in 1993, transforming it into a major force behind global artificial intelligence systems. Industry observers note that whoever permanently succeeds him will hold immense influence over the burgeoning technology sector, given Nvidia's dominant role supplying advanced hardware.

Candidates under consideration include key internal product directors as well as executives from prominent semiconductor partners. Kress, who joined Nvidia in 2013 after extensive corporate finance experience, could also remain in the role long-term.

US Envoys Hold Peace Discussions in Moscow and Dublin

B1+ · 1 min

American diplomatic representatives Jared Kushner and Steve Witkoff have conducted new talks with Russian President Vladimir Putin in Moscow before traveling to Dublin to meet Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky. The diplomatic push aims to find a structural path toward resolving the ongoing war.

The meetings follow preliminary discussions held in the United Arab Emirates regarding European security arrangements. However, significant political gaps remain, with Russian demands far exceeding conditions that Ukraine considers acceptable. US officials indicated that European nations will be invited to participate directly in upcoming formal sessions.

During the talks, President Zelensky raised fresh operational concerns regarding satellite connectivity interruptions affecting Ukrainian defensive operations against airborne targets. American representatives confirmed that resolving technical barriers remains essential to facilitate stable communication and build constructive dialogue.

Meta Rolls Out Advanced Advertising and AI Tools for Thai Merchants

B1+ · 1 min

Technology firm Meta has launched new digital advertising tools in Thailand designed to help businesses manage marketing across multiple commerce platforms. Thailand is among the first markets in the Asia-Pacific region to gain access to these solutions.

The new features include an omnichannel system—meaning an approach that connects physical stores with digital channels—allowing companies to measure combined online and offline purchases. Another tool uses generative artificial intelligence to help merchants turn product images or short descriptions into video advertisements in minutes.

Meta representatives explained that Thai consumers frequently research products on social media before purchasing at physical shops. The tools aim to bridge this gap, helping small businesses create content faster while accurately tracking their sales results across all sales locations.

Community-Led Housing Projects Support Low-Income Urban Residents

B1+ · 1 min

More than half a million disadvantaged residents across Asia have gained safe housing through community-led initiatives, according to industry expertise shared at the Sustainability Expo 2026. The approach, known as Collective Housing, puts residents at the center of planning and constructing their own neighborhoods.

Advocates from Thailand, Indonesia, and Myanmar shared practical examples of how local groups can empower families to upgrade settlements. In Thailand, projects along Khlong Prem Prachakorn—a khlong being a traditional canal running through the capital—allowed residents to improve living conditions while staying in their original neighborhoods.

Speakers highlighted that allowing communities to manage local savings and coordinate construction creates stronger, more adaptable cities. Rather than relying solely on government aid, organized neighborhoods can rebuild faster after natural disasters and maintain long-term urban stability.

Navigating Local Food Markets Abroad with Confidence and Ease

B2 · 2 min

Exploring a bustling neighborhood market in a new country is one of the most rewarding aspects of international travel. From vibrant morning stalls to aromatic night venues, local food hubs offer direct insight into culinary traditions. However, stepping into unfamiliar food markets can occasionally feel overwhelming for travelers unsure of local etiquette or ordering customs.

To enjoy these experiences fully, baseline preparation is helpful. Before visiting, research local meal times and customary ordering procedures. Observing how local residents queue, select items, and pay provides clear guidance on vendor expectations. Paying attention to these subtle interactions helps visitors avoid awkward misunderstandings and ensures a cordial interaction for both buyer and merchant.

Communication at food stalls does not require fluency in the local language. Basic polite phrases, paired with clear gestures, work remarkably well. Pointing respectfully to displayed ingredients or choosing dishes prepared fresh in front of customers helps ensure safety while simplifying the process. Managing money logistics carefully by carrying small cash denominations also speeds up transactions during busy market hours, preventing unnecessary mayhem at crowded stalls.

Food hygiene is another key practical consideration. Selecting stalls with high customer turnover generally ensures fresh ingredients and avoids a stunning drop in quality. Watching for clean workspace practices provides additional reassurance. Embracing local dining habits with reasonable preparation transforms simple meals into memorable cultural exchanges.

Thai Speaker's Trap

Direct translation from Thai

Direct Translation: 'Eat Water' for Drinking

I am very thirsty after walking, so I want to eat water.

I am very thirsty after walking, so I want to drink water.

Thai speakers often translate the general daily verb 'กิน' (kin) directly into English as 'eat', which frequently leads to awkward phrasing like 'eat water' (กินน้ำ). In standard English grammar, the verb 'eat' is reserved strictly for solid food items, whereas liquid beverages always require the verb 'drink'. Using the correct verb ensures your spoken English sounds natural, clear, and professional in social settings.
