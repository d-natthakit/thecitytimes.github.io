Historic NYC Thanksgiving Parade Postponed to Saturday Due to Severe Storm

C1 · 3 min

New York City’s world-famous Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade has been postponed to Saturday as a powerful coastal storm threatens to bring heavy rain and dangerous wind conditions. Organizers were forced to adjust their schedule after forecasters warned of sustained winds reaching up to 25 mph and even stronger gusts. Official safety guidelines dictate that the event's massive floating character balloons must remain grounded if sustained winds top 23 mph or gusts exceed 34 mph.

The decision marks the very first time in the parade's century-long history that the event has been postponed because of bad weather. In the past, the iconic street celebration was suspended entirely during World War Two from 1942 through 1944 to save essential materials like rubber and helium for the war effort. The giant inflatables were also kept on the ground due to high winds in 1971, but the parade itself went ahead.

Ahead of the storm, New York Governor Kathy Hochul declared a state of emergency for New York City, Long Island, and Westchester County. Executive producer Will Cross expressed disappointment at the delay but emphasized that the safety of performers, volunteers, and millions of spectators remains the absolute priority. To protect the inflatables from severe weather impediments, workers deflated 100 giant balloons on Wednesday night and moved them to a secure facility in neighboring New Jersey until they can be reinflated.

The parade began in 1924 as a promotional stunt for Macy’s Manhattan flagship store, initially featuring live animals borrowed from the Central Park Zoo. Air-filled and helium balloons replaced the live animals a few years later. Organizers anticipate that this year’s rescheduled 100th annual parade will showcase 10 new character inflatables, alongside a special musical tribute to Dolly Parton led by Megan Hilty and a 10-minute Broadway musical medley before Santa Claus closes the show.

Ukraine Strikes Russian Airbase East of Moscow in Drone Attack

B1+ · 1 min

Ukrainian forces launched a successful long-range strike against the Savasleyka airbase in Russia's Nizhny Novgorod region, damaging five military aircraft. The attack targeted Russian MiG-31 jets, which carry hypersonic missiles, and Su-34 fighter-bombers used in regular strikes on Ukrainian cities.

Kyiv stated the operation was part of a broader attempt to weaken Moscow's military assets and disrupt ongoing missile launches. Russian officials have not yet commented on the strike, which follows several recent Ukrainian actions aimed at Russian shipping and infrastructure.

The attack comes as fighting continues to escalate across Eastern Europe. Ukrainian officials insisted they will continue striking military targets until the campaign against their nation's energy system stops.

Brad Pitt and Angelina Jolie End Years of Legal Disputes Over French Vineyard

B1+ · 1 min

Hollywood actors Brad Pitt and Angelina Jolie have reached an out-of-court settlement, ending an eight-year legal battle over their shared French winery, Château Miraval. Representatives for both stars confirmed the agreement in a joint statement, asking for privacy for their family.

The legal fight began after Jolie sold her share in the vineyard to a international beverage group without Pitt's knowledge. Pitt sued, arguing they had agreed not to sell without mutual permission, while Jolie filed counter-claims regarding a private flight incident in 2016.

The agreement resolves all remaining claims between the former couple, bringing an end to a high-profile courtroom stalemate. However, separate litigation between Pitt and the spirit company that bought Jolie's stake remains ongoing.

Thailand Today

Rajadamnern Stadium Announces Billion-Baht Boxing Arena in Phuket

B1+ · 1 min

Bangkok's famous Rajadamnern Muay Thai Stadium is expanding outside the capital for the first time by building a one-billion-baht arena at Central Phuket. Set to open in early 2029, the complex will feature a 2,500-seat stadium, a combat sports museum, and dining areas.

The project is a joint venture between Central Pattana and Global Sport Ventures. Organizers hope the new venue will tap into the growing global interest in traditional Thai martial arts and attract one million international visitors to the island each year.

International architecture firms known for designing major international stadiums will handle the project. The venue will host live fights, cultural shows, and evening concerts alongside Central Phuket's expanded luxury shopping zone.

Police Wait Outside Hat Yai Bathroom to Arrest Suspected Online Scam Mule

B1+ · 1 min

Police officers in Hat Yai waited nearly 30 minutes outside a bathroom door to arrest a 22-year-old suspect accused of operating a mule account. Officers watched the suspect enter the toilet and stayed silent while he watched social media videos inside.

When the suspect finally emerged, police arrested him under a warrant issued by the Nakhon Sawan Kwaeng Court for computer fraud. The suspect had allegedly sold his bank account for 300 baht, which scammers then used to steal tens of thousands of baht from victims.

The suspect admitted he knew about the case but had failed to report to police earlier. Officers took him into custody and arranged his transfer to face formal legal proceedings.

Creating a Sustainable Home: Simple Habits for Every Household

B2 · 2 min

Adopting an environmentally friendly lifestyle at home does not require expensive upgrades or a complete life overhaul. Small, thoughtful actions practiced consistently can significantly lower household waste and reduce energy consumption without disruption to your daily routine.

A great starting point is managing kitchen waste effectively. Instead of tossing organic scraps into standard rubbish bins, households can turn food waste into rich soil through basic home composting. Furthermore, learning how to creative reuse leftovers helps households scavenge extra value from food purchases, saving money while cutting back on unnecessary trash.

Energy management is another vital area where small changes yield big results. Simple habits like unplugging electronics when not in use, switching to energy-efficient light bulbs, and utilizing natural sunlight can noticeably lower monthly electric bills. Taking control of daily power consumption allows families to maintain comfort while lessening their environmental footprint.

Reducing plastic consumption requires households to relinquish the habit of using single-use items. Carrying reusable shopping bags, storing food in glass containers, and choosing products with minimal packaging drastically reduces long-term landfill contribution. Making deliberate choices at the grocery store sends a clear signal to manufacturers about eco-friendly demand.

Under close scrutiny, green living proves to be both cost-effective and rewarding. Protecting the vitality of our local environment starts with everyday decisions inside our living rooms and kitchens. By taking small, manageable steps today, every family can help build a cleaner community for the future.

Real English · Two Takes

Should households compost their organic kitchen waste at home?

C1 · 2 min

Essential for Sustainability

Home composting is an effective way to cut household waste and support urban gardening. While it takes time to set up, it keeps organic matter out of landfills. Although some people fear bad odors, a properly maintained bin stays fresh and clean. Managing kitchen scraps locally reduces city transport emissions. With basic guidance, any resident can navigate household composting easily and turn everyday food scraps into rich, healthy soil.

Impractical for Modern City Life

Composting at home sounds wonderful, but it is often unsuited for apartment living and busy schedules. Even with careful maintenance, food scraps in small spaces frequently attract pests like fruit flies. It seems clear that municipal collection services are much more efficient for dense neighborhoods. Rather than forcing individuals to manage decomposing waste indoors, cities should provide centralized collection facilities for convenience.

Argument Phrases

  • While it takes time to set up — concession
  • Although some people fear bad odors — concession
  • It seems clear that — hedging

Thai Speaker's Trap

Countable/uncountable & plurals

Using 'Garbage' or 'Trash' as Plural Nouns

We should reduce our home garbages by composting food scraps.

We should reduce our home garbage by composting food scraps.

In English, 'garbage' and 'trash' are uncountable nouns. Thai speakers often add an 's' because they think of individual items of trash (ขยะ). To talk about multiple items, use 'types of garbage' or simply keep 'garbage' without an 's'.
