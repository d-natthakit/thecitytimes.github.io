US Seeks End to War in Ukraine as Trilateral Abu Dhabi Talks Resume

C1 · 3 min

United States officials announced they are pursuing a final end to the war in Ukraine during trilateral talks in Abu Dhabi. The high-level discussions bring together representatives from Russia, Ukraine, and the United States for their first substantive negotiations since February. While leaders Volodymyr Zelenskyy and Vladimir Putin were absent, negotiators opened a multi-day schedule aimed at resolving Europe's largest military conflict since the Second World War.

The renewed diplomatic push comes during a shifting domestic and international political landscape. Recent midterm elections in the United States have weakened President Donald Trump at home, while Russia's wartime economy faces increasing pressure. Simultaneously, Moscow has intensified strikes against Ukraine's power grid as winter sets in. American representatives Jared Kushner and Steve Witkoff led the delegation in the United Arab Emirates to revive dialogue after three previous negotiation rounds yielded no major breakthroughs.

A central element of the American proposal involves designing a new security architecture for Europe. US officials suggested reviving the NATO-Russia Council, suspended after the 2022 invasion, and exploring security guarantees for Ukraine similar to NATO's Article 5 protection. Under such a framework, Washington could provide long-term funding, resources, and direct military support. To secure a deal, Washington also indicated that it might consider lifting economic sanctions against Russia.

European leaders, however, remain cautious about the evolving negotiations. The European Union's foreign policy chief, Kaja Kallas, stressed that international agreements protecting national sovereignty already exist, but Russia fails to respect them. Furthermore, European officials expressed concern that Washington might grant major concessions to Moscow, such as agreeing to Russian demands that NATO reduce its footprint in Eastern Europe. Critics also warned that hybrid warfare and cyberattacks could complicate any prospective security guarantees.

To address European anxieties, US Secretary of State Marco Rubio assured allies in Paris that European nations would participate in the broader process. European representatives held separate talks with Ukrainian and American teams, issuing a joint statement that any credible agreement must respect territorial integrity and independent decision-making. Ukrainian President Zelenskyy noted that initial discussions covered humanitarian concerns, including prisoner exchanges and the return of captive civilians, while cautioning that significant work remains ahead.

World in Brief

US House Republicans Block Defense Secretary Impeachment Vote

B1+ · 2 min

Republicans in the United States House of Representatives have blocked an attempt to impeach Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth. Lawmakers voted 211 to 204 to send the resolution to the Republican-led House Judiciary Committee. This procedural step effectively stops a direct vote on eight articles of impeachment. Representative Thomas Massie was the sole Republican who joined Democrats to oppose the referral.

The impeachment resolution accused the Pentagon chief of leading an unauthorized war against Iran without required congressional approval. It also cited military strikes against alleged drug trafficking vessels, which legal scholars labeled extrajudicial actions. Hegseth dismissed the resolution as a farce and an attempt at political sabotage, while President Donald Trump publicly praised the Republican unity that defeated the motion.

The vote highlights growing political tension following recent midterm election losses for the ruling party. Polls indicate voters blame the ongoing conflict for rising fuel and food prices. Although the resolution was halted, critics continue to question Hegseth's strategy and the accuracy of public updates regarding the war effort.

Thomas Markle Returned to Custody After Violating Release Terms

B1+ · 2 min

Thomas Markle, the father of Meghan, Duchess of Sussex, has been taken back into US federal custody in Florida. A judge revoked his pretrial release after prosecutors reported that he removed his electronic location monitor. Markle was denied bond during an appearance before a magistrate judge in Tampa, who ordered a medical evaluation and necessary medication for the 82-year-old.

Markle was originally arrested following FBI searches of Los Angeles storage units he rented since 2008. Federal agents seized items that contained alleged child sex abuse material, with witnesses identifying Markle in older photographs. Markle has strongly denied the charges, calling the allegations sick lies. Officials emphasized that court orders regarding pretrial detention are strict rules that will be fully enforced.

Prior to his arrest, Markle lived in the Philippines before returning to the United States. Following the judge's determination that his housing arrangements were untenable, Markle will remain in custody while legal proceedings continue in federal court.

Thailand Today

Biofuel Consumption Surges After Government Tax Reductions

B1+ · 1 min

Biofuel usage across Thailand has expanded significantly following recent government tax incentives. According to Prime Minister Anutin Charnvirakul, daily consumption of E20 gasohol jumped nineteen percent after the cabinet slashed excise taxes on blended fuels. Consumption of B20 biodiesel also saw a dramatic surge of forty-one percent during the same period.

The three-month tax reduction policy plays a pivotal role in lowering fuel costs for vehicle owners while supporting local farmers. Higher demand for B20 helps absorb palm oil surpluses, while increased E20 usage boosts consumption of cassava and molasses. Government officials stated that the measure encourages cleaner fuel choices, reduces dependence on crude oil imports, and stabilizes domestic crop prices.

Energy officials noted that the sharp rise in biofuel uptake strengthens the agricultural economy. By encouraging motorists to adopt alternative fuels, the temporary tax policy provides direct financial support to rural farming communities across the nation.

Former Monk Leads Vehicle Protest Demanding Cheaper Diesel

B1+ · 1 min

A former Buddhist monk turned village headman has led a vehicle convoy from southern Thailand to Bangkok to demand lower fuel prices. Mano Khrueakaew, a 52-year-old local administrator from Krabi province, organized the demonstration to highlight how high fuel costs harm transport operators, small businesses, and agricultural workers struggling with rising living costs.

The protest vehicle column grew to approximately 80 vehicles upon reaching Government House in Bangkok. Mano is calling on the administration to cut diesel prices by ten baht per liter and review national fuel pricing calculations. He emphasized that high fuel expenses severely impact rubber farmers and crop harvesting operations during challenging seasonal periods.

Mano's public campaign has drawn widespread attention through social media. This latest action follows his previous advocacy work against industrial waste and proposed regional infrastructure projects, reflecting a broader public effort to demand economic relief for local communities.

Feature · Arts & Entertainment

The Evolving World of Voice Acting in Video Games and Animation

B2 · 2 min

The entertainment industry has undergone a massive transformation in how stories are brought to life. Voice acting, once seen as a background element in animated cartoons and early video games, has matured into a sophisticated craft. Today, voice performers are celebrated artists who bring depth, emotional complexity, and distinct personalities to digital characters, shaping how audiences connect with modern entertainment.

Technological advancements have elevated the demands placed on voice actors. Modern video game production routinely uses performance capture technology, requiring actors to deliver spoken dialogue while wearing motion-tracking suits. This means performers must master physical movement alongside vocal delivery. The process demands exceptional focus, as actors must recreate subtle emotional beats within minimal studio environments, often interacting with imaginary props and virtual scenery.

Beyond technical skills, voice artists must demonstrate incredible vocal flexibility to portray nuanced character personalities. A single actor might voice a battle-hardened warrior, a quirky comic relief character, or a sinister villain within the same project. Maintaining vocal health while executing intense sound effects, such as shouting or recording combat grunts, requires disciplined technique. Without proper training, performers risk severe vocal strain during long recording sessions.

The rise of global streaming platforms and localized media has created new opportunities for voice talent worldwide. Professional studios now seek authentic regional accents and diverse vocal styles to match international character rosters. Increased industry transparency regarding casting decisions and union protections has also helped improve working conditions for performers across the globe.

As interactive storytelling continues to expand, voice performance remains an essential bridge between human emotion and digital art. For fans and aspiring creators alike, appreciating the hard work behind the microphone offers a richer understanding of contemporary media production.

Thai Speaker's Trap

Tenses & time expressions

Using 'Since' Instead of 'For' with Duration

We have discussed this issue since two hours.

We have discussed this issue for two hours.

In English, use 'for' to describe a total duration or length of time (e.g., for two hours, for three days). Use 'since' only when referring to a specific starting point in the past (e.g., since 9:00 AM, since last Monday). Thai speakers often confuse these because the word ตั้งแต่ (tang-tae) or เป็นเวลา (pen-wee-la) can lead to mixing up starting points and total durations.
