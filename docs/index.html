Nvidia Opens Inquiry into CEO Jensen Huang Over Relationship with Suspected Spy

C1 · 3 min

United States chipmaker Nvidia has launched an independent internal investigation into its chief executive officer, billionaire Jensen Huang, after he confirmed having an extramarital relationship with a woman accused of gathering intelligence for China. On Sunday, Nvidia announced that the inquiry would be led by a committee of independent directors supported by outside legal counsel. While the board stated it has no reason to believe commercial secrets or national security were compromised, the probe will thoroughly evaluate all accessible details.

The board emphasized that it prioritizes national and economic security as paramount concerns for shareholders, while maintaining full confidence in Huang and his management team. Huang will remain at the helm of the semiconductor giant during the proceedings, having committed his full cooperation. In an internal message sent to employees, Huang admitted to unfaithfulness toward his wife, expressing deep remorse for hurting his family and acknowledging that no excuse justified his personal conduct.

The corporate inquiry follows revelations that Jensen Huang may have been targeted in a foreign espionage plot involving Alice Huang, also known as Huang Le. Although she shares the same surname, she has no family relation to the executive. Alice Huang served as an executive at Megaspeed, a Singapore-based data center operator that emerged as a major customer by purchasing roughly 4.8 billion dollars in Nvidia hardware. American authorities had previously monitored her over suspected chip smuggling to China, eventually assessing that she functioned as an intelligence asset passing commercial data to Beijing. The Chinese Embassy in Washington, DC, denied all spying claims.

The matter quickly drew intense political scrutiny in Washington, where relations between the two economic superpowers remain strained over artificial intelligence. A bipartisan group of lawmakers expressed alarm regarding the flow of advanced American processors abroad. Representatives on the House foreign affairs and intelligence panels have called on Huang to appear for a closed-door briefing on November 18. Lawmakers, including Senator Elizabeth Warren, stressed that Congress must establish whether sensitive technological assets were exposed, renewing pushes for stricter export curbs on advanced hardware.

The controversy comes at a delicate juncture for Nvidia, whose valuation surged into the trillions of dollars as its chips became the bedrock for major generative artificial intelligence developers. The company has also contended with volatile market conditions following recent United States midterm elections. Last week, Nvidia shed 450 billion dollars in equity value in a single session before recovering modestly. As geopolitical competition intensifies around strategic computing infrastructure, corporate governance and executive integrity across the technology sector face growing public and governmental oversight.

Deadly Train Collision in Egypt Leaves Seven Dead and Scores Injured

B1+ · 2 min

At least seven people were killed and more than one hundred injured when two passenger trains collided near Giza, south of Cairo. The crash occurred when an active southbound service crashed into the rear of a stationary train on the tracks. The impact caused several carriages to overturn and plunge down a railway embankment.

Emergency teams rushed to the site, where rescue workers searched through mangled cars for trapped survivors. Dr. Khaled Abdel Ghaffar, Egypt's health minister, confirmed that local hospitals were placed on heightened alert to treat fractures and head trauma. A train driver was among those confirmed dead, and prosecutors warned the fatality count could increase.

President Abdul Fattah al-Sisi ordered an investigation into the collision and promised to hold any negligent parties accountable. Railway operations between Giza and Upper Egypt were suspended while heavy cranes cleared the wreckage. The incident has renewed public demands for systemic rail safety improvements and prompted calls for the transport minister to resign.

Pope Leo Visits Argentina Amid Sharp Clashes Over Austerity Policies

B1+ · 2 min

Pope Leo XIV arrived in Argentina on Monday, visiting the Cottolengo Don Orione, a Catholic care facility that provides a sanctuary for approximately 400 individuals with disabilities. The stop marked the pontiff's first full day of an official tour across South America, which also includes Uruguay and Peru.

The visit highlights stark ideological disagreements with Argentine President Javier Milei. Since taking office, Milei has slashed state spending to combat inflation, freezing funding for vital medications, food, and diapers for disability institutions. Church representatives noted that the pope's message champions human dignity, directly contrasting with the president's free-market approach.

Economic tensions remain elevated throughout the country as social groups protest significant losses in retiree purchasing power. Furthermore, the leaders diverge over technological governance; the pope previously urged limits on artificial intelligence, whereas Milei advocates for data center expansion without restrictive regulations. Observers view the trip as a profound test of state and church relations.

Thailand Today

Advisors Urge Year-End Investors to Look Beyond Tax Deductions

B1+ · 2 min

As the final months of 2026 approach, financial planners are advising Thai investors to consider broader financial goals rather than simply chasing tax savings. Many taxpayers are currently assessing options like Retirement Mutual Funds (RMFs), Thai ESG funds, and qualifying life insurance products to reduce taxable income before the tax year closes.

Advisors stress that an investor's risk tolerance, long-term timeline, and liquidity requirements should guide these decisions. RMFs allow deductions up to 30 percent of assessable income within a 500,000-baht collective retirement ceiling, but they demand holding assets until age 55. Similarly, Thai ESG funds offer an individual deduction ceiling of 300,000 baht under specific multi-year holding conditions.

Purchasing financial products solely to exhaust deduction allowances can lead to costly penalties if investors fail to meet strict holding periods. Professionals suggest that taxpayers carefully calculate remaining limits to find an optimum balance between protection, investment stability, and legitimate tax relief.

Bangkok Police Secure Government House Ahead of Vehicle Protest

B1+ · 1 min

Metropolitan police have established security perimeters across Bangkok ahead of an approaching convoy of roughly 1,000 vehicles. The demonstrators, led by Mano Khrueakaew, a kamnan (a subdistrict headman) from Krabi, are traveling from southern provinces to demand a 10-baht reduction in fuel prices and state relief for living expenses.

Authorities enacted street closures around major junctions near Government House, declaring a 50-meter perimeter around the compound as an off-limits assembly zone through November 20. National police leadership instructed deployed officers to enforce public assembly regulations strictly yet proportionately, designating specific parking zones to limit traffic congestion for everyday commuters.

Safety concerns rose after law enforcement discovered a licensed handgun with ammunition in a participant's vehicle during a transit stop in Phetchaburi. The protest leader confirmed he had explicitly prohibited firearms. Police plan to monitor the assembly closely to maintain public order.

Recharging the Desk-Bound Professional: Small Shifts for Daily Energy

B2 · 2 min

Modern office life often requires spending eight to ten hours seated before computer monitors, processing endless emails and attending back-to-back virtual discussions. Over time, this sedentary routine drains physical vitality and clouds mental sharpness. Many working adults assume that restoring focus demands intensive workouts or drastic dietary overhauls. In reality, introducing modest, intentional behaviors throughout the workday can dramatically revive both physical endurance and mental clarity.

The primary drain on daytime energy is prolonged physical stillness, which slows blood circulation and reduces oxygen delivery to the brain. To counter this mid-afternoon slump, professionals can integrate brief physical interruptions into their work patterns. Standing during phone conversations, pacing while brainstorming, or completing gentle leg stretches every hour can reactivate physical circulation. These minimal adjustments combat muscular stiffness and restore mental alertness without disrupting productive workflows.

Equally critical is the management of sensory input and digital strain. Continuous exposure to bright monitors triggers ocular fatigue, leading to tension headaches and cognitive exhaustion. Adopting regular visual breaks, such as gazing at distant objects across the room for twenty seconds, provides the optic nerves with necessary rest. Pair this visual habit with conscious breathing intervals, exhaling slowly to trigger the nervous system's relaxation response and clear accumulated tension.

Finally, professional performance depends on mindful hydration and deliberate boundary-setting during breaks. Rushing through lunch while replying to workplace messages prevents cognitive recovery, increasing fatigue later in the afternoon. Stepping away entirely from screens during meal breaks enables true psychological recovery, helping to foster steady energy reserves. By committing to these practical, sustainable micro-habits, professionals can safeguard their daily vitality and sustain professional effectiveness throughout demanding schedules.

Thai Speaker's Trap

Articles (a / an / the)

Articles (a / an / the): Omitting Articles Before Singular Countable Nouns

I need to send email to client before leaving office.

I need to send an email to the client before leaving the office.

In Thai, nouns do not require determiners, so speakers often omit 'a', 'an', or 'the' when translating thoughts directly from phrases like ส่งอีเมลให้ลูกค้า (song email hai look-kha). In English, singular countable nouns like 'email', 'client', and 'office' cannot stand alone without an article or determiner. Use 'an' before vowel sounds for non-specific items, and 'the' when referring to a specific person, place, or thing known to both speaker and listener.

Error Identification

  1. The supervisory board launched an inquiry to investigate the chief executive's personal conduct. — 'Investigate' is a transitive verb and takes a direct object without 'about'.
  2. Prosecutors announced plans to charge the negligent driver with manslaughter after the collision. — Correct as edited; avoid using incorrect prepositions such as 'charge for'.
  3. The government slashed social spending despite angry rallies organized across major cities. — 'Spending' is an uncountable noun when referring to financial expenditure.
  4. Officers were instructed to respond proportionately to the scale of the gathering. — The adverb 'proportionately' collocates with the preposition 'to', not 'with'.
  5. Workers stuck in sedentary jobs should pay close attention to their daily posture. — The idiom is 'pay attention to', not 'pay attention on'.
