Cover Story

Huawei Convicted of Trade Secrets Theft but Acquitted of Racketeering Charges

C1 · 2 min

A United States federal jury has found Chinese telecommunications giant Huawei guilty of conspiracy to steal trade secrets and bank fraud following a two-month legal trial in Brooklyn. However, jurors acquitted the firm of the most serious charge it faced: engaging in a criminal racketeering conspiracy. That charge, often applied in organized crime proceedings, argued that Huawei operated systematically as an illicit enterprise.

The legal battle originated in late 2018 following the arrest of senior executive Cathy Meng in Canada under a US warrant. The US government placed Huawei on a trade restriction list shortly afterward, alleging that the firm's growth posed national security risks. Prosecutors claimed during the trial that Huawei spent decades using false statements and theft to secure an unfair market advantage, including breaching international rules by doing business in Iran and North Korea.

In response, defense lawyers maintained that the firm achieved its international presence across 170 countries through genuine innovation and hard work rather than unlawful activity. Following the verdict, the defense expressed satisfaction that the jury rejected the racketeering count, characterizing that specific charge as an excessive overreach by federal authorities. Defense representatives indicated that they plan to pursue every available appeal after sentencing takes place in late 2027.

Despite years of heavy legal scrutiny and trade restrictions from Washington, industry experts note that the criminal trial has not halted Huawei's broader commercial operations. The company has since successfully diversified its technology investments, transforming itself into a major player in artificial intelligence development across Asian markets.

The partial conviction marks a historic moment in corporate litigation involving foreign technology companies operating in the US. While federal prosecutors emphasized that corporate misconduct will face legal accountability, the mixed ruling leaves both sides preparing for protracted appellate arguments over the coming years.

US Army Prepares Controlled Explosion of Explosive Dust in Iowa

B1+ · 1 min

The United States Army is scheduled to detonate 68 barrels containing five tons of toxic lead azide at an ammunition plant in rural Iowa. Operational commanders stated that immediate action was necessary to avoid a far more dangerous spontaneous explosion of the unstable chemical compounds.

Military authorities have positioned physical barriers around the site to capture airborne particles, but officials warned nearby residents to prepare to evacuate if necessary. Emergency alerts instructed citizens living within twenty miles to remain indoors with closed windows as wind forecasts help direct the projected plume path.

Local officials and environmental groups strongly criticized federal agencies for failing to communicate early health risks to vulnerable populations. State leaders promised to investigate the administrative delays once formal legislative sessions open in the new year.

South Africa Sentences Foreign National Attempting to Join Russian Military

B1+ · 1 min

A South African court has convicted a Zimbabwean national who was arrested at a Johannesburg airport while attempting to board a flight to Russia. The individual admitted he applied online to fight in the Ukraine conflict in exchange for financial payment.

The court handed down a five-year suspended sentence alongside an order for immediate deportation back to Zimbabwe. He pleaded guilty under national laws that prohibit mercenaries and strictly regulate foreign military assistance.

Authorities across Africa continue investigating online recruitment networks targeting young jobseekers. Governments in Kenya and Zimbabwe report that hundreds of citizens have been enticed abroad under false promises of civilian employment.

Maritime Authorities Prepare Emergency Recovery of Tanker off Koh Samui

B1+ · 1 min

Thai maritime agencies are preparing to salvage the Unique 10, a large liquefied gas tanker that ran aground on coastal rocks off Koh Phaluai in Surat Thani province. Aerial inspections confirmed that the vessel's hull suffered damage, but no oil leakage has been detected in surrounding waters.

A secondary tanker has been dispatched to receive the grounded ship's ammonia cargo before recovery tugboats move the damaged vessel. Officials will deploy protective floating containment booms around the site to safeguard marine conservation zones during the operation.

Marine research centers will conduct detailed environmental assessments of nearby coral reefs once the vessel is safely towed to a regional shipyard for structural repairs.

Feature · Arts & Entertainment

The Art of Constructive Critique: Navigating Feedback in Creative Projects

B2 · 2 min

In modern professional life, working on creative projects—whether designing a marketing campaign, crafting a corporate video, or writing software documentation—requires frequent collaboration. However, evaluating artistic output or creative ideas often feels subjective. Unfiltered feedback can easily bruise egos, while overly vague praise fails to improve final results. Mastering how to give and receive constructive critique is an essential skill for any workplace team.

Effective feedback begins with establishing clear objectives before any creative work starts. When reviewers evaluate a draft against agreed goals rather than personal taste, the discussion remains objective. Instead of stating that a design looks unappealing, effective professional communicators point out specific elements that may not resonate with the intended target audience. Framing comments around outcomes helps team members process suggestions without feeling personally attacked.

Timing and structure also play a crucial role in maintaining productive working relationships. Critique should be offered in stages rather than delivered all at once right before a deadline. Early discussions ought to focus on high-level concepts and structure, saving minor formatting details for final reviews. This layered approach prevents creators from wasting effort refining ideas that might ultimately be rewritten.

Receiving feedback requires active listening and emotional balance. Professionals should view notes not as personal criticism, but as valuable insights designed to elevate the project. Asking clarifying questions helps identify the underlying cause of a reviewer's concern, allowing the creator to propose creative solutions that satisfy project requirements without compromising technical quality.

Ultimately, building a culture of healthy critique transforms feedback sessions into collaborative problem-solving opportunities. When teams separate personal identity from creative output, project quality improves and workplace trust grows naturally.

Thai Speaker's Trap

Tenses & time expressions

Using 'Since' Instead of 'For' with Time Spans

We have discussed this project since two hours.

We have discussed this project for two hours.

Thai speakers often use 'since' for all time expressions because the Thai word ตั้งแต่ (tang-tae) can refer to starting points. In English, use 'since' for a specific starting point in time (e.g., since 9:00 AM) and 'for' when referring to a total duration or duration length (e.g., for two hours).
