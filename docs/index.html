Cover Story

Eisenkot Handed Mandate to Form Israeli Government

C1 · 3 min

Israeli President Isaac Herzog has officially announced his plan to task opposition leader Gadi Eisenkot with establishing a new coalition administration. The move follows an inconclusive general election held on October 27, which left neither Eisenkot nor current Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu with an outright legislative majority. Eisenkot, leader of the centrist Yashar party and former military chief of staff from 2015 to 2019, will now receive a 28-day statutory window, along with a possible 14-day extension, to assemble a working government.

The political arithmetic in the 120-seat Knesset remains fiercely competitive. Netanyahu's right-wing Likud and its allied religious-nationalist partners captured 53 seats, maintaining the single largest bloc. Meanwhile, Eisenkot's centrist formation alongside three allied secular and centrist opposition factions secured 48 seats. Despite trailing in total seats, Eisenkot secured 67 recommendations for the premiership from incoming lawmakers during consultations with the presidency, compared to 46 for Netanyahu, granting him the initial mandate to break the national political impasse.

A pivotal factor in this process is the historic electoral showing of Arab-majority parties. The United Arab List and the Joint List together attained an unprecedented 19 seats amid elevated voter turnout, positioning their leaders as potential kingmakers. In his talks with Herzog, Eisenkot received formal endorsements from both Arab-led lists. However, forming a durable coalition with their direct participation presents significant hurdles, given that almost all Jewish parliamentary factions previously committed not to enter a formal governing alliance with them.

President Herzog emphasized the urgent need for pragmatism to prevent repeated elections, noting that factions must recognize that not every past promise can be maintained under present conditions. Historical parallels underline this challenge, as Israel endured five national votes between 2019 and 2022 amid intense rivalry. Observers note possible feasibility pathways, including an emergency unity government pairing Eisenkot and Netanyahu, defectors joining a conservative bloc, or agreements involving ultra-Orthodox or Arab representatives.

In his initial reaction on social media, Eisenkot stated that the moment requires an emergency administration, pledging to prove worthy of public trust. Nevertheless, negotiations will test whether Israeli party leaders can resolve deep policy divides and overcome institutional friction before the legal deadline expires.

Airstrikes Near Yemeni Capital Claim Over Twenty Lives

B1+ · 2 min

Airstrikes hit the village of al-Shariya near Yemen's capital, Sanaa, on Saturday, killing at least 22 people and wounding 30 others, according to Houthi health officials. Rebel spokespersons stated that the bombardments hit multiple residential homes, blaming Saudi Arabia for conducting the assault. Footage broadcast on local channels showed emergency teams clearing rubble while treating victims in regional hospitals.

The attack follows a fragile security environment in Yemen after a yearslong truce collapsed earlier this summer. Fighting between Iranian-backed Houthi forces and the internationally recognized government, supported by Saudi Arabia, has escalated sharply. Intense combat continues along strategic Red Sea coastal hubs, alongside missile exchanges directed across borders.

While Saudi officials did not immediately comment on the strike, coalition forces reported intercepting a separate missile fired toward Riyadh province. With United Nations data recording rising casualties and displacements, international observers warn that escalating military allegations and direct strikes threaten to intensify broader regional confrontation.

Tens of Thousands Join French Rallies Over High Living Costs

B1+ · 1 min

More than 140,000 demonstrators gathered throughout France on Saturday for a fourth consecutive weekend of nationwide protests concerning inflation and rising living costs. While the Interior Ministry counted 146,000 participants, labor unions calculated nationwide attendance at 250,000, including 75,000 marchers in Paris alone.

The demonstrations, which originally began over poor resources in high schools, have expanded to include workers demanding higher wages and better public services. French authorities deployed roughly 70,000 security officers nationwide. In Paris and Lyon, law enforcement used tear gas to disperse groups that blocked thoroughfares or damaged public property.

Tensions remain high as upcoming national elections approach. Trade unions reported gaining organizational momentum, while government ministers warned that security forces will maintain active vigilance against unauthorized street violence and disruptions in the coming weeks.

Thailand Today

Longtime Overstayer Arrested in Bangkok Drug Raid

B1+ · 1 min

Immigration authorities have detained a 63-year-old German national in northern Bangkok after discovering he had overstayed his visa by more than 15 years. Police arrested the individual on Friday at a residential property in Bang Khen district, discovering a packet of crystal methamphetamine in his possession.

The police operation began following persistent community complaints regarding neighborhood disturbances. Residents reported loud late-night gatherings and frequent visitors arriving on motorbikes in the Phahonyothin area. Authorities investigated these reports, discovering the resident had exceeded his legal stay by 5,732 days.

The suspect was transferred to Bang Khen police station for legal processing. He faces formal charges for unlawful overstaying as well as possession of Category 1 narcotics for personal use, which will trigger deportation proceedings after judicial resolution.

Asian Consumers Embrace Individual Scents and Wellness Rituals

B1+ · 1 min

Asian consumers are moving away from having a single signature perfume, opting instead to build flexible scent wardrobes that match individual feelings, occasions, and environments. This behavioral transformation incorporates diverse lifestyle items, including home aromatherapy, wellness oils, and hair mists.

Younger shoppers in Japan, South Korea, and China are driving this market shift. Many individuals purchase personal fragrances on a monthly or weekly basis. Rather than relying on prestigious international labels, buyers seek nuanced storytelling, sustainable local botanicals, and neutral scent profiles that prioritize personal wellbeing over overt brand status.

Regional projections indicate the commercial market for premium fragrances will expand significantly by 2026. Retailers are adapting their product lines to streamline personal discovery formats and experiential shopping, satisfying customer demand for emotional connection and authentic identity.

Feature · Business / Money

The Hidden Cost of Price Inconsistencies at Retail Checkouts

B2 · 2 min

In contemporary consumer environments, retail transactions happen at unprecedented speed. Point-of-sale terminals, digital payment apps, and automated stock systems handle thousands of transactions every minute. However, operational speed does not always guarantee accuracy. When shelf tags fail to match the price scanned at the cash register, businesses face significant reputational and financial risks.

Most billing errors originate from subtle administrative delays rather than intentional deception. In fast-paced retail shops with high product turnover, promotional campaigns frequently shift. Store staff may update digital inventories before replacing physical shelf tags, or promotional discounts might expire without prompt physical adjustments. Even a small price discrepancy can quickly erode hard-earned customer trust.

From an operational standpoint, billing inaccuracies disrupt cashier workflows. When a patron challenges a charge, staff must halt lines to confirm details, frustrating everyone waiting. Modern business studies reveal that shopper friction at checkout often leads to lost loyalty. Many consumers will simply patronize competitors rather than return to a vendor perceived as careless with basic pricing.

Retailers must adopt proactive auditing methods to prevent these hidden losses. Forward-thinking companies conduct daily shelf-to-register audits and deploy mobile scanners that verify price tags instantly. Regular staff training regarding refund protocols ensures that any billing disputes receive immediate and fair solutions.

Clear communication serves as the final safeguard for retail integrity. Displaying store price-matching policies and honoring displayed shelf labels without hesitation builds enduring consumer confidence. By treating checkout accuracy as an essential customer service priority rather than a minor technical detail, modern enterprises protect both immediate revenue and lifetime customer loyalty.

Thai Speaker's Trap

Confusing word pairs (e.g. bored/boring)

Confusing 'Price' and 'Prize'

The cashier told me that the prize of this jacket was reduced today.

The cashier told me that the price of this jacket was reduced today.

Thai speakers sometimes confuse 'price' (the monetary cost of buying something) with 'prize' (an award won in a competition or contest), as Thai often uses terms like 'ราคา' (rakha) for cost and 'รางวัล' (rangwan) for awards interchangeably with similar English loanwords. Remember that goods in a store carry a 'price,' whereas a contest winner receives a 'prize.'
