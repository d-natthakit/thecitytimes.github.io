COP31 Climate Summit Ends in Deadlock as Nations Reject Final Draft Agreement

C1 · 3 min

The COP31 climate conference in Antalya, Turkey, has officially concluded without an agreement after days of intense negotiations ended in a complete stalemate. The annual gathering became the longest-running summit in the history of the UN event, extending two full days past its scheduled conclusion on Friday before negotiations broke down on Sunday afternoon. Participating delegations from across the globe ultimately failed to achieve the mandatory consensus required to adopt the central policy text, leaving the future direction of international climate action uncertain.

The primary cause of the sudden collapse was a deep division over how to address fossil fuels, which remain the largest driver of global climate change. The European Union, the United Kingdom, and several developing nations rejected the final draft agreement prepared by the joint presidencies of Turkey and Australia. Representatives argued that the proposed text failed to meet the urgency of the global climate crisis because it completely omitted any commitment to phase out or transition away from coal, oil, and gas.

Speaking on behalf of the EU, Ireland's environment minister stated that the bloc would not endorse a decision that failed to respond to scientific reality or that risked widening the disparity between global commitments and necessary environmental action. EU delegates openly accused several major oil-producing states of hijacking the talks to protect commercial interests. In response, Russian representatives defended their opposition to including explicit emission-reduction targets, while leveling direct criticism at European leaders for their rigid negotiation stance.

The proposed Antalya pact called on countries to electrify their national economies and urged wealthy nations to provide adaptation finance to poorer regions. It also called for minimizing temperature overshoots relative to the Paris Agreement goals. However, the proposal faced sharp criticism for promoting carbon capture and storage technologies, which many delegates argued would simply allow fossil fuel consumption to continue unabated under a different name.

To prevent the complete collapse of the entire conference, organizers managed to pass minor decisions on secondary agenda items, such as supporting workers in high-carbon industries during economic shifts. However, the presidency formally acknowledged that the main cover decision was not adopted. The culmination of these unsuccessful negotiations leaves international climate diplomacy at a critical deadlock as countries face divergent paths forward.

Suspected Drone Strike Hits Russian Navy Vessel in Libyan Port

B1+ · 1 min

A Russian naval vessel stationed at the Benghazi naval base in eastern Libya was hit by a suspected drone attack early Sunday morning. Satellite imagery and geolocated videos confirmed that the Steregushchiy-class corvette caught fire and largely capsized in the harbor, leaving only its forward bow visible above water.

According to local authorities, the warship had docked to collect fuel and supplies on its return route to Russia under an existing bilateral agreement. Officials reported no casualties from the warship itself, though a nearby commercial vessel was also targeted, causing minor injuries to crew members who were promptly treated at a local medical facility.

Eastern Libyan authorities strongly condemned the strike, describing it as a direct violation of maritime safety and national sovereignty. An investigation is currently underway in coordination with Russian counterparts to identify who carried out the attack before tensions escalate further.

US Democrats Secure Historic House Majority Following Midterm Recount

B1+ · 1 min

United States Democrats have expanded their majority in the House of Representatives to at least 258 seats, marking the party's largest sweep in the chamber since 1992. The milestone was confirmed on Sunday after a Republican representative conceded following a formal recount in North Carolina.

The victory establishes a divided government in Washington, with Democrats controlling both the House and Senate while a Republican president occupies the White House. Emboldened Democratic leaders announced plans to focus their upcoming legislative agenda on lowering living costs for households while increasing oversight of administration policies.

Only four House seats remain undecided, with two races in Louisiana moving to a runoff next month. While Republicans must relinquish key committee chairmanships, Democratic strategists stated that the election results reflect strong voter demand for immediate economic policy adjustments.

Thai Tech Sector Reports Solid Ninth-Month Earnings Growth

B1+ · 1 min

Major technology companies listed on the Stock Exchange of Thailand have reported impressive financial results for the first nine months of the year. Retailer COM7 reported a significant earnings increase, supported by high consumer demand for modern mobile devices and expanding electric vehicle fleet operations. Meanwhile, tech distributor Synnex experienced strong revenue growth after government agencies and private firms upgraded commercial hardware to maintain operational efficiency.

Industry executives noted that while consumer purchasing power in some product categories remains constrained, enterprise technology investments remain stable. Companies plan to optimize their product portfolios to navigate ongoing market challenges and prevent plans from beginning to unravel before year-end.

Thailand and Cambodia Hold Maritime Boundary Talks in Malaysia

B1+ · 1 min

Thailand and Cambodia will hold a third round of conciliation talks in Kuala Lumpur this week under the United Nations Convention on the Law of the Sea. The four-day meeting aims to address a long-standing maritime boundary dispute in the Gulf of Thailand.

The discussions follow earlier preparatory rounds held in Singapore and The Hague, which established procedural frameworks and confidence-building measures between the two nations. A five-member international conciliation commission will facilitate the technical talks alongside high-level diplomatic and legal delegations from both countries.

The maritime dispute concerns an overlapping claims area of approximately 26,000 square kilometers. Thai officials hope the structured international process will help establish common ground and relieve diplomatic friction surrounding boundary technicalities.

Protecting Daily Energy: Practical Steps to Beat Physical and Mental Fatigue

B2 · 2 min

In fast-paced modern workplaces, maintaining physical energy and focus throughout the day can feel like a constant struggle. Many professionals experience a sharp drop in momentum during the afternoon, leading to decreased productivity and heightened stress. Understanding how to sustain personal vitality is essential for long-term career success and daily personal well-being.

A common cause of sudden exhaustion is poor morning nourishment. Relying exclusively on high-sugar snacks or immediate caffeine boosts can cause rapid spikes in blood glucose, followed by a severe energy crash. Choosing balanced meals rich in complex carbohydrates and protein ensures that nutrient energy is slowly absorbed by the body, keeping your physical engine running smoothly.

Physical movement also plays a crucial role in maintaining sharp mental performance. Remaining seated at a desk for several hours continuously reduces blood circulation and increases muscular tension. Short active breaks, such as walking for five minutes every hour, help stimulate blood flow, refresh the mind, and reduce the lethargy associated with desk-bound routines.

Hydration is another critical factor that busy workers frequently overlook. Even mild dehydration can impair concentration, lower mood, and trigger persistent headaches. Keeping a refillable water bottle at your workstation serves as a constant visual reminder to drink fluids steadily throughout the day rather than drinking large quantities only when feeling extremely thirsty.

Finally, managing psychological load is just as important as managing physical health. When daily work tasks pile up, taking brief, intentional breaks allows your brain to reset and avoid burnout. Establishing healthy boundaries between professional duties and rest ensures sustainable performance over time.

Thai Speaker's Trap

Articles (a / an / the)

Omitting Articles Before Countable Nouns

I sent report to manager yesterday morning.

I sent the report to the manager yesterday morning.

Thai speakers often omit articles because the Thai language does not use words like 'a', 'an', or 'the' before nouns. In English, singular countable nouns like 'report' or 'manager' must have an article or determiner when referring to specific items under detailed scrutiny.
