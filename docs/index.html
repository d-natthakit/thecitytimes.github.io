New York City Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade Postponed Due to High Winds

C1 · 3 min

New York City’s annual Macy’s Thanksgiving Day parade has been postponed due to excessively windy weather and will now be held on Saturday instead of Thursday. Forecasters say an impending storm on Thursday may spoil the street party, which is traditionally marked by a march of huge inflatable balloons among the city skyscrapers and historic downtown areas.

AccuWeather is forecasting sustained winds of 12 to 25 mph and rain later on Thursday. Under safety guidelines, large balloons must stay grounded if sustained winds exceed 23 mph or gusts exceed 34 mph. The parade balloons have been suspended only once previously because of weather, back in 1971, creating a rare historical precedent for the organizers.

NBC quoted Macy’s executives as saying that the safety of participants and spectators is the top priority for everyone involved. The giant balloons will be deflated on Wednesday night and stored securely in neighbouring New Jersey until they are reinflated for the rescheduled Saturday celebration, ensuring that no potential accidents occur during the severe weather event.

This year the parade will include a vibrant tribute to Dolly Parton led by Megan Hilty, along with special appearances by Melissa Joan Hart and other stars leading the way for Santa Claus. Nine Broadway shows will be featured as part of a ten-minute medley instead of full show numbers, providing a delightful musical experience for the millions of viewers tuning in from home.

The Macy’s parade began in 1924 to create buzz for the department store holiday windows at Herald Square. Organizers borrowed animals from the Central Park Zoo for the early parades but ended the practice in 1927 after children became frightened by the live creatures, leading to the introduction of the famous inflatable novelties that we see today.

The parades were fully cancelled previously only in 1942, 1943, and 1944 because valuable materials needed for the balloons were diverted for the World War Two effort. The modern holiday tradition returns this weekend after meticulous planning, and local authorities are working to ensure a smooth rescheduling process for all attendees and international visitors.

Local businesses and tourism boards anticipate that shifting the event to Saturday will still generate significant economic activity for the city. Hotels, restaurants, and retail stores near the parade route have adjusted their staffing schedules to accommodate the massive crowds expected over the weekend, turning a potential weather disruption into a proactive opportunity for local commerce.

Ukraine Strikes Russian Airbase

The General Staff stated that three MiG-31 jets and two Su-34 fighter-bombers were impacted in the latest long-range attack. Russia did not immediately comment on the report provided by the military command.

The operation follows recent strikes in Libyan waters and the Gulf of Finland, as well as damage to the Crimea bridge. Fighting has continued since the full-scale invasion in February 2022, causing ongoing regional instability.

B1+ · 1 min

Phuket to Host Major Muay Thai Stadium

B1+ · 1 min

Rajadamnern Muay Thai Stadium plans to open its first venue outside Bangkok at Central Phuket in early 2029. The project represents an investment of more than one billion baht as operators aim to attract one million tourists annually.

The complex will cover nine thousand square metres and accommodate about two thousand five hundred spectators. It will feature live bouts, cultural shows, and a museum alongside dining and retail outlets.

Global Sport Ventures and Central Pattana are collaborating on the expansion to promote the traditional sport. Architecture firms Populous and Nikken Sekkei are involved in designing the new combat sports venue.

Feature · Home / Environment

Creating an Eco-Friendly Home Environment for Daily Well-Being

B2 · 2 min

Modern urban living often disconnects us from natural rhythms, making home environmental design more important than ever. Transforming our living spaces into sustainable sanctuaries requires intentional choices rather than drastic lifestyle overhauls. By focusing on small daily habits, residents can substantially reduce their ecological footprint while improving indoor air quality and mental clarity in their living spaces.

A pivotal step involves optimizing natural light and ventilation throughout the apartment or house. Heavy drapes can be replaced with sheer fabrics that invite daylight in, reducing the need for artificial daytime lighting. Indoor plants also serve as natural air purifiers, adding vitality to living areas while helping to regulate indoor humidity levels naturally. Selecting durable, locally sourced furnishings further minimizes environmental impact over time.

Energy management plays an equally crucial role in household sustainability as urban areas continue expanding. Switching to energy-efficient LED lighting and unplugging idle electronic devices prevents unnecessary power consumption during peak hours. Furthermore, establishing a comprehensive indoor sorting system for organic waste and recyclables encourages mindful consumption habits among all household members, fostering a cleaner community.

Ultimately, building an eco-friendly home is a gradual journey of awareness rather than an overnight transformation. Residents should focus on progressive improvements that align with their personal routines and household budgets. These thoughtful adjustments ultimately foster a healthier living space for both current occupants and future generations, ensuring a flourishing ecosystem at the residential level.

Real English · Two Takes

Should urban households adopt strict indoor composting habits?

C1 · 2 min

Composting Benefits the Environment

Composting organic kitchen waste significantly reduces the volume of garbage sent to municipal landfills. While managing food scraps indoors requires some initial effort, the positive impact on local soil health and waste reduction is undeniable for modern cities.

Practical Challenges Limit Adoption

Indoor composting can attract unwanted pests and create unpleasant odours in compact apartments. Although the environmental intentions are good, practical limitations make strict composting systems unsuitable for many busy city residents who lack outdoor space.

Thai Speaker's Trap

Countable/uncountable & plurals

Using 'Garbage' or 'Waste' as a Countable Noun

Please throw away these garbages before leaving the office.

Please throw away this garbage before leaving the office.

In English, words like 'garbage', 'waste', and 'equipment' are uncountable nouns. They do not take an 's' at the end and require singular verbs. Thai speakers often translate 'khaya', which can feel collective, into a plural form, but you must use uninflected forms like 'some garbage' or 'this waste'.
