Cover Story

Mexican Elite Forces Arrest Alleged Cartel Boss 'El Chapo Isidro' in Fierce Sinaloa Raid

C1 · 3 min

In a high-stakes military operation on Friday night, Mexican elite special forces captured Fausto Isidro Meza Flores, widely known as 'El Chapo Isidro'. The alleged drug kingpin was listed among the FBI's Ten Most Wanted fugitive figures. The raid took place in Guasave, a city located in the northwestern state of Sinaloa, and sparked fierce clashes between security personnel and heavily armed cartel operatives.

According to Mexico's defence ministry, the arresting force arrived in the target area using stealth and surprise before establishing a defensive perimeter. Suspected cartel gunmen immediately retaliated with gunfire and explosive devices. Despite the intense onslaught, troops entered the residence and secured Meza Flores alongside his alleged second-in-command, Germán N, alias 'Serafín', and an alleged financial operator named José N. Eight suspected criminals were detained in total.

The overnight assault triggered severe local unrest across Guasave. Cartel members erected burning roadblocks, while frightened civilians fled gun battles that interrupted an ongoing baseball game at Kuroda Park stadium. Official reports confirmed four Mexican infantry soldiers lost their lives during the fighting, while seven cartel attackers died. Meza Flores was subsequently transported by military aircraft to Mexico City to face formal legal proceedings.

United States intelligence assisted Mexican authorities during the mission. United States President Donald Trump had recently pressed Mexican President Claudia Sheinbaum to combat criminal syndicates more aggressively, warning that cartels could not be allowed to run the neighbouring country. Although Meza Flores shares a famous alias, he is not related to Joaquin 'El Chapo' Guzman. Instead, officials identify him as the head of the Beltrán Leyva criminal organisation and an ally of the Mayos faction within the Sinaloa Cartel.

Meza Flores had managed to evade justice for well over a decade despite an active reward of up to five million dollars offered by the United States Department of State. A federal indictment issued by United States prosecutors in 2012, followed by another in 2019, accused him of conspiring to distribute vast quantities of heroin, methamphetamine, cocaine, and marijuana. In Mexico, he faced outstanding warrants concerning organised crime and public health violations.

The arrest marks a major operational development in regional security, although security analysts caution that leadership vacuums often trigger volatile internal power struggles among rival factions. Mexican defence authorities confirmed that Meza Flores remains subject to an active extradition order to the United States. While Sinaloa state authorities announced that local order had been largely restored by Saturday, the broader cross-border legal process will now commence.

Tens of Thousands Rally Across Europe to Protest Soaring Living Costs and Housing Pressures

B1+ · 1 min

Tens of thousands of demonstrators gathered across major European cities on Saturday to voice mounting frustration over unaffordable housing, rising living costs, and funding reductions for public services. The mobilisations brought diverse civic groups together, ranging from student collectives to established trade unions.

In France, the interior ministry recorded over 82,000 marchers nationwide, marking the sixth consecutive weekend of unrest sparked initially by school conditions. Meanwhile, Portuguese workers marched through Lisbon amid heavy rain. Unions highlighted severe housing strain, noting domestic property prices have more than doubled since 2017 while wages trail the European Union average.

Similar youth-led demonstrations took place in Germany, Finland, and Italy, addressing broader concerns from education spending to military service. With household budgets under strain, community organisers confirmed that public campaigns demanding affordable essential goods and fair pay will continue across several countries next week.

Thailand Today

Bangkok Opens New Khlong Skywalk Connecting Thon Buri and Talat Phlu Heritage District

B1+ · 1 min

The Bangkok Metropolitan Administration has officially unveiled a dedicated pedestrian skywalk spanning Khlong Bangkok Yai, a major historic canal in the capital. Bangkok Governor Chadchart Sittipunt presided over the opening ceremony on Saturday morning along the canal bank.

Measuring 93 metres long and three metres wide, the bridge creates a direct pedestrian route between Bangkok Yai district office and the vibrant Talat Phlu neighbourhood in Thon Buri. The structure includes dual passenger lifts, safety ramps, and stairs to ensure it remains fully accessible for elderly residents and people with mobility challenges.

City officials noted the crossing separates foot traffic from adjacent roadways, enhancing commuter safety. It also provides panoramic views of the Phra Buddha Dhammakaya Thep Mongkol statue at Wat Pak Nam Phasi Charoen, linking urban transit hubs with historic cultural sites to boost community tourism.

European Council Proposes Digital Levy on Global Tech Giants to Bridge Budget Deficit

B1+ · 1 min

European Council President Antonio Costa presented a comprehensive spending plan on Saturday that seeks to resolve heated internal disputes among member governments regarding long-term European Union finances. The proposal significantly scales back earlier European Commission budget figures.

To generate fresh public revenue, the plan introduces a three percent levy on online advertising and digital services. This measure specifically targets technology firms reporting an annual European digital turnover of at least 50 million euros and worldwide revenues surpassing 750 million euros, functioning as a non-traditional intermediary fiscal contribution.

The broader funding package also incorporates higher tobacco duties and an electronic waste levy. European leaders will debate the contentious proposal during upcoming Brussels summits before entering formal negotiations with the European Parliament ahead of upcoming regional elections.

Feature · Business / Money

Rethinking Workplace Cash Flow: Practical Budgeting Systems for Modern Professionals

B2 · 2 min

Managing personal finances alongside professional responsibilities often feels overwhelming in an era dominated by automated transactions, variable earnings, and persistent economic pressures. Many working adults maintain a steady income yet struggle to identify where their earnings vanish by the end of each month. Mastering personal capital requires intentional structure rather than constant deprivation.

A dependable financial strategy begins with a precise spending audit. Tracking expenses over a standard thirty-day cycle reveals unexamined habits, such as recurring digital subscriptions or premium workplace conveniences, which quietly erode monthly savings. Categorising expenditures into fixed necessities, personal development, and savings enables clearer decision-making before impulse purchases take hold.

To build financial resilience, experts often suggest using a deliberate fund allocation model. Instead of treating savings as leftover money, professionals should direct a predetermined percentage toward emergency funds or retirement instruments the moment compensation arrives. This practice creates a tangible safety net that mitigates anxiety during career transitions, medical emergencies, or unexpected market downturns.

Furthermore, individuals should treat personal financial health as an ongoing business operation. Reviewing investment balances, renegotiating banking charges, and adjusting budgets quarterly ensures fiscal strategies adapt to rising expenses. By treating income management with systematic professionalism, individuals can build durable economic independence and achieve genuine peace of mind.

Thai Speaker's Trap

Confusing word pairs (e.g. bored/boring)

Confusing 'Borrow' and 'Lend'

Could you please borrow me your pen for a moment to complete this form?

Could you please lend me your pen for a moment to complete this form?

Thai speakers often confuse these words because the Thai verb ยืม can express both directions of giving and receiving temporary possession. In English, 'borrow' means to receive something temporarily from someone, while 'lend' means to give something temporarily to another person.
