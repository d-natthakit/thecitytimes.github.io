# TASK: STEP 2 — WORD BANK, LANGUAGE LAB, OTHER LANGUAGES
The articles are finished. You get each of today's words in the sentence where it appeared, and today's headlines. About 700 words in total.

# FIELDS
- word_bank: one entry for every word given, in the given order (★ words first, then ↺ words). Each: word (exactly as given), part of speech, a short learner-friendly English definition that fits today's sentence, Thai translation (Thai script), and collocations: 3 common collocations for ★ words, an empty list for ↺ words.
- activity: the plan's activity. Exactly 6 items, instructions, and 6 answers in the same order. Use 6 different ★ words (plus a ↺ word if it fits). Write NEW sentences, not copies of today's sentences. Formats:
  - Collocation match: each item is "word — ?"; list the 6 jumbled partner words in the instructions.
  - Gap fill: each item is a sentence with one gap written as ______ ; put the word box in the instructions.
  - Word forms: a sentence with a gap ______ and the base word in brackets, e.g. "(decide)".
  - Synonym choice: a sentence with the target word written as **word**, then "(a) ... (b) ... (c) ..."; choose the closest meaning.
  - Mini crossword clues: no grid. Each item is a clue with the letter count, e.g. (8), or (5, 2) for two words.
  - Odd one out: a target word followed by 4 words; one does not collocate with it. Explain briefly in the answer.
  - Context clue challenge: a short sentence with the word as **word**, then "(a) ... (b) ... (c) ..."; guess the meaning from context only.
  (Brackets are allowed in activity items when the format needs them.)
- errors: exactly 5 sentences about today's headlines, each with exactly ONE error typical at B2–C1 (articles, prepositions, tense, word form, collocation, register). Try to use the ★ words not used in the activity. Give the corrected sentence and a reason of a few words. Check that each original really has one error and the correction fixes only that.
- other_language: about 150–180 words in English, for the plan's language. Focus on words people really use. Basic, accurate, well-known items only; keep correct accents.
  • theme_intro: 1–2 sentences introducing today's everyday theme.
  • phrases: 3 everyday words, phrases or short sentences for the theme (original, simple English-style sound guide, English meaning).
  • root: one word root from the language (or its Latin/Germanic source) with its meaning, 2–4 English words that come from it, and a one-sentence note.
  • borrowed: one word English has borrowed from this language, with a one-sentence note on its original meaning.
  • curious: one interesting, fun or hard-to-translate word, with pronunciation, meaning and a one-sentence note.
  Do not repeat anything in the plan's "already used" list.
